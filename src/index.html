Quest Schedule Exporter

Highlight your entire schedule on Quest, copy, and paste it below to generate a Google Calendar (.ics) file.

How to import into Google Calendar:

  1. Open Google Calendar.
  2. Click the gear icon (Settings) in the top right.
  3. Select Settings > Import & export.
  4. Select the downloaded .ics file and choose the calendar you want to add the events to.
  5. Click Import.
