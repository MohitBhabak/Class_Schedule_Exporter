📅 How to import into Google Calendar:

  1. Open Google Calendar.
  2. Click the ⚙ Settings gear icon in the top right > Settings.
  3. In the left sidebar, click Import & export.
  4. Click Select file from your computer and choose the downloaded schedule.ics.
  5. Select your target calendar (or create a new "Classes" calendar) and click Import.
